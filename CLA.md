# Contributor licence agreement

This is the agreement you enter into when you contribute code, documentation
or anything else to kaviri. It is short on purpose. If a sentence here is not
clear to you, open an issue and ask, because an agreement you cannot explain
is not worth signing.

the person or the company sending the contribution. "Contribution" means
anything you send the project: code, patches, documentation, examples,
scripts, test fixtures, designs.

royalty-free copyright licence to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative

## 6. Signing

Sign once and it covers every contribution you make afterwards.

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it. Signed: <your full name> <your email>
```

If you are signing on behalf of a company, say which company on the same line
and be the person entitled to do that.

If any part of this agreement turns out to be unenforceable, the rest of it
still stands.
